# SahiDaam demo video script — under 3 minutes

Record at localhost:3000, 1080p, no facecam needed. Screen-record with mic.

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook
> "Indians overpay for gadgets every day — not because deals don't exist, but
> because the real price is scattered across ten tabs. SahiDaam fixes that:
> paste a link, get every Indian seller's price, the price history, and a
> straight BUY or WAIT verdict."

Show the homepage hero.

## 0:15–0:50 — Live search
Type "Sony WH-1000XM5", hit Check price. Narrate the loading steps.
> "It pulls live Google Shopping data via SerpApi — Amazon, Flipkart, Croma —
> then runs deterministic verdict math. No AI guessing."

## 0:50–1:20 — Verdict + sellers
Show the verdict card: BUY NOW, deal score 69, "16% below 30-day median".
Scroll the seller table, then the "Best time to buy" hint.
> "Best price ₹21,990 at Flipkart versus a street median of ₹23,240.
> One junk listing — a ₹1,499 case — got filtered automatically. And the timing
> engine reads the price trend to tell you whether waiting saves more."

## 1:20–1:50 — Price history
Show the chart.
> "Every lookup saves a snapshot, so the chart proves today is actually a deal —
> not just a strikethrough MRP trick."

## 1:50–2:20 — Photo search
Open "identify from a photo", paste an image URL.
> "No link? Drop a photo — Google Lens identifies the product, then the same
> pipeline runs."

## 2:20–2:40 — Vs mode (the wow shot)
Switch to the "Vs mode" tab, click the demo matchup.
> "Sony XM5 versus Bose QC45 — two live verdicts, one winner. This is how
> you settle the group-chat debate with data."

## 2:40–2:55 — Trending drops
Switch to "Trending drops", hit scan.
> "And every morning, SahiDaam hunts today's biggest genuine drops itself —
> ranked by real discount against price history, not inflated MRP claims."

## 2:55–3:00 — Close
Back to homepage.
> "SahiDaam — Commerce & Market Intelligence track. Next.js, SQLite, three
> SerpApi engines, and verdict math you can audit in one file. Repo link below."
